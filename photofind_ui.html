Photo Finder

Find any photo by describing it.

Type things like “birthday cake” or “dog in the snow” and Photo Finder shows the matching photos. It works entirely on this computer, so your photos stay private.

There are people in your photos that Photo Finder doesn’t know yet. Tell it who they are, and you can find every photo of them.

Who is this?

When new faces turn up, Photo Finder will ask again. People you’ve named are recognized automatically in new photos.
